Find the exit

You are lost in the sewers with a flashlight. Four of thirteen entities hunt you each run. The metal-floored vents belong to the Wired; the open courtyards belong to the Longman. They notice light and noise, then lose interest if you stay quiet and out of sight. Find the green exit door.

Paused

Items: walk over a pickup to collect it. 1 battery, 2 bottle (thrown where you look), 3 glow stick, 4 adrenaline. Click a slot on a phone. WASD to walk. The mouse is locked to the view while you play, so move it to look around (Esc or P releases it and pauses), click or F for the flashlight, Shift to run. Arrow keys left and right also turn. A lit flashlight and running both get you noticed from farther away, and standing still is silent. P pauses, M mutes. On a phone use the pad to walk, drag the view to look, and the Light and Run buttons.

Field notes

Four of these thirteen are picked at random every run. Ones you have met this run are outlined.
